Advisory work involves sensitive material. This page sets out the principles HESSARA applies to that material. The [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) covers personal data in more detail.

## Principles

### Your data remains yours

Documents and conversations in a workspace belong to the organisation that supplied them. They are used to provide the service to that organisation.

### Access is limited to those who need it

Access to a workspace should be restricted to the people its owner has invited, and to the minimum of operational staff required to keep the service running.

### Reasoning is inspectable

Recommendations should be traceable to the sources they rest on, so that an organisation can audit how a conclusion was reached.

### You can leave with your data

An organisation should be able to export its material and to have it deleted when it stops using the service.

## Questions to settle before you begin

Whatever platform you use for advisory work, obtain clear written answers to the following. We aim to publish ours here.

| Topic | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Storage | Where is data stored, and in which jurisdiction? |
| Retention | How long is data kept, and how is deletion confirmed? |
| Training | Is customer data used to train or improve models? |
| Access | Which staff can reach customer data, and under what controls? |
| Encryption | How is data protected in transit and at rest? |
| Sub-processors | Which third parties process data, and for what purpose? |
| Incidents | How and when are customers told about a security incident? |

## Your part

- Share only the documents a question needs.
- Review who has access to each workspace regularly.
- Treat exported reasoning and reports as confidential documents.
