Programs are the routes by which organisations, researchers, and partners can work with HESSARA more closely than through the platform alone.

## Early access

For organisations that want to trial new models and advisory features before general release, and are willing to share structured feedback in return.

**Suited to:** teams with a concrete decision problem and the time to evaluate tools properly.

## Research collaboration

For academic and independent researchers studying reasoning, evaluation, and the use of AI in decision-making.

**Suited to:** researchers with a defined question and a plan to publish their findings.

## Partner program

For consultancies and integrators who want to bring HESSARA into their own client work.

**Suited to:** firms with established advisory practices and clients in regulated or complex fields.

## How to apply

Tell us which program interests you and what you hope to do through the [contact page](/contact/). We will reply with next steps once the program terms are published.
