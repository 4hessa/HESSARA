This policy explains what personal information HESSARA collects, why, and what choices you have. It applies to this website and to the HESSARA platform.

## Information we collect

- **Information you give us:** such as your name, email address, and organisation when you contact us or create an account.
- **Workspace content:** documents and conversations you add to the platform in order to use it.
- **Usage information:** basic technical data such as pages visited, device type, and approximate location derived from an IP address.

## How we use it

- To provide and maintain the service
- To respond to your enquiries
- To keep the service secure and prevent misuse
- To understand how the service is used so that it can be improved

## What we do not do

We do not sell personal information.

## Sharing

Information is shared only with service providers that help us operate the service, under agreements that restrict their use of it, or where the law requires disclosure.

## Retention

Personal information is kept for as long as it is needed for the purposes above, and is then deleted or anonymised.

## Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to object to certain uses. To make a request, use the [contact page](/contact/).

## Cookies

This website uses only the cookies and similar technologies needed for it to function, unless you are told otherwise and asked for consent.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted on this page.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the [contact page](/contact/).

See also [Security, privacy, and data handling](/resources/security/) and the [Terms & Usability](/terms/).
