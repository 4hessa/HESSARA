HESSARA builds advisory tools for people who have to make difficult decisions and explain them afterwards. We are interested in hearing from people who care about that problem.

## What the work involves

- **Reasoning systems:** models and agents that argue, cite, and admit uncertainty
- **Evidence and retrieval:** making sure an answer rests on the right passage of the right document
- **Product and design:** interfaces that make reasoning legible to the person relying on it
- **Advisory practice:** methods for using these tools well in real organisations

## What we look for

- Care for accuracy over impressiveness
- The habit of stating what you do not know
- Clear writing
- Respect for the person on the other side of the screen
